How overload builds, and how stress is brought back down

This view shows the core feedback structure behind stress and dysregulation in autistic people. Load from the setting raises stress. Sustained stress wears down the capacity to cope. Rest, retreat, self-regulation, meltdown and shutdown bring stress back down, some at a cost. View 2 covers masking, burnout and support. View 3 covers the environment in detail.

Every link has been checked against published research and given an evidence grade. Select a + or − sign on the diagram to see the evidence and sources for that link. The full review is in the evidence review report.
