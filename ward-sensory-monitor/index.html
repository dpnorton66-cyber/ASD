Readings refresh every 30 seconds. Levels for each area are set by the ward's occupational therapist and reviewed monthly. Sensors count people and measure sound pressure, movement and illuminance; they do not record speech or images. Patients and staff are told apart by the staff badge readers at each sensor, not by identifying anyone.
